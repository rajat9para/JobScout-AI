STEP 8: SETUP UPTIMEROBOT (CRITICAL — DO NOT SKIP)
================================================================================

[ ] 8.1 Create Monitor
      → In UptimeRobot Dashboard, click "Add New Monitor"
      • Monitor Type: HTTP(s)
      • Friendly Name: JobScout Keep Alive
      • URL: https://jobscout-v2-web-xxxx.onrender.com/health
      • Monitoring Interval: Every 5 minutes (300 seconds)
      • Click "Create Monitor"

[ ] 8.2 Why This Matters
      → Render free web services SPIN DOWN after 15 min idle
      → Without UptimeRobot, the web service sleeps
      → UptimeRobot pings /health every 5 min keeping it alive
      → This is the ONLY way to make free tier reliable

================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING QUICK FIXES
================================================================================

Problem: "No digest email received"
Fix: Check Brevo API key is valid. Check sender email is verified in Brevo.
     Check spam/junk folder. Try /trigger-digest endpoint.

Problem: "Brevo says 'sender not verified'"
Fix: Go to Brevo → Settings → Senders → Add & verify your sender email.

Problem: "Empty PDF every night"
Fix: Check profile interests match available jobs. Try BULK mode by
     widening interests. Check scraper logs for extraction issues.

Problem: "Database connection error"
Fix: Check SUPABASE_SERVICE_KEY (not anon key). Re-run schema.sql.

Problem: "Gemini rate limit exceeded"
Fix: Wait 1 minute. Reduce scrape frequency to 2 hours in render.yaml.

Problem: "PDF generation error"
Fix: Check reportlab is installed. Check logs for specific error.
